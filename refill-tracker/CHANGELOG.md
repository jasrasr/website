# Changelog

## 1.5.1 — 2026-11-21

- Show a public Refill Tracker landing page with links to shared sign-in and account registration.

## 1.5.0 — 2026-11-21

- Enter a receipt with multiple inventory items and save the updates together.
- Calculate sales tax with an editable 8% default and per-line tax exemptions.

## 1.4.0 — 2026-11-21

- Log optional seller, pack size, purchase quantity, and total cost with refills.
- Convert packs to inventory units and show the cost per unit in refill history.

## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-21

- Add an optional household starter list with distinct 2% and 4% milk entries.
- Add editable item details and preserve stock and history when an item is renamed.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-21

- Record who opened or replaced an item and who refilled its stock.
- Show refill history with quantities, dates, and storage locations.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-21

- Track storage balances, transfers, and opening history by room.
- Estimate household usage and suggest when to order more.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-21

- Add private household inventories using 1-Framework and shared User Management.
- Add editable item details, one-tap quantity adjustments, and an automatic restock list.
